Retrieval record
----------------

Every row on this page was confirmed against the DOI resolver, the publisher page
or the OpenAlex record. Entries without a DOI are cited by a stable URL: the
Scientific American article, the Andrews–Curtis Conjecture Challenge, and the
reward-hacking workshop paper. Two anchors are not carried because no resolvable
record was found for them: Rourke 1979 and a move-count paper attributed to Bridson.
Bridson's distortion paper is cited instead.

Ivanov's equivalence changes the standing of one hypothesis in the corpus. The cyclic
setting is not an assumption but a published equivalence, so the corpus cites it as the
licence for the cyclic framework rather than as background.

A rejected identifier
---------------------

The OpenAlex index returns a different Andrews–Curtis paper from 1965,
*Some spaces whose product with E¹ is E⁴* (`10.1090/s0002-9904-1965-11394-5`), by
the same authors. It is not the source of the conjecture and is not cited as
such. The conjecture source is the *Free Groups and Handlebodies* entry above.
